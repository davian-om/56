Mac Operating System(MOS)

The Mac Operating System (macOS) is the operating system developed by Apple Inc. for Macintosh computers. It provides a simple, fast, and secure environment for users to perform different tasks such as creating documents, browsing the internet, watching videos, editing photos, and programming. macOS has an easy-to-use graphical user interface (GUI) with features like the Finder, Dock, and Spotlight Search, which make navigation simple. It is known for its excellent performance, strong security, regular updates, and smooth integration with other Apple devices such as the iPhone and iPad. Because of its stability and user-friendly design, macOS is widely used by students, professionals, and creative artists.
