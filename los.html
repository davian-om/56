Linux Operating System(LOS)

Linux Operating System (Linux OS) is a free and open-source operating system developed by Linus Torvalds in 1991. It is based on the Unix operating system and is widely used in computers, servers, smartphones, and supercomputers. Linux is known for its stability, security, and high performance. It supports multitasking, multiple users, and a wide variety of software applications. Because its source code is open, developers can modify and distribute it according to their needs. Popular Linux distributions include Ubuntu, Fedora, and Debian. Linux is widely used by programmers, businesses, and organizations because it is reliable, flexible, and does not require a license fee.
