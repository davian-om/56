Android Operating System(AOS)

Android Operating System (Android OS) is a mobile operating system developed by Google. It is mainly used in smartphones, tablets, smart TVs, and wearable devices. Android is based on the Linux operating system and is open-source, allowing developers to create different types of applications. It provides a simple and user-friendly interface with features such as touch-screen support, multitasking, internet browsing, GPS, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and access to millions of apps through the Google Play Store. Android is one of the most popular operating systems in the world because it is easy to use, highly customizable, and supports a wide range of devices.
